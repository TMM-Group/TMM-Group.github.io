Open call for undergraduate researchers, graduate students and postdoctoral fellows

English summary of the recruitment announcement. Chinese original

Undergraduate research

Students from all undergraduate years in mechanics, physics, materials science, and related fields are welcome. Projects include atomic-scale imaging, building an atomic force microscope with LEGO, scientific programming in C, Fortran, and Python, machine learning, high-performance computing, and materials databases.

After training, students can use lab instruments and apply for computing access. Members may join weekly lab meetings, a monthly English-language journal club, and group activities. If you are interested, contact one of the researchers below.

Graduate study

We welcome applications for master’s and direct PhD programs from undergraduate students in mechanics, physics, materials science, and related disciplines. Master’s students interested in micro- and nanoscale mechanics, multiphysics, mechanical metamaterials, functional materials, or energy materials can apply through the doctoral application and assessment process. We welcome applicants with theoretical and experimental interests.

Postdoctoral fellowships

Applicants should hold or be nearing completion of a PhD in mechanics, physics, materials science, or a related field. They should be able to conduct independent research and collaborate effectively, and must meet BIT’s postdoctoral eligibility requirements.

Preferred backgrounds include first-principles, phase-field, or coupled-field finite-element methods; neutron scattering or synchrotron techniques; fabrication and characterization of ferroic or thermoelectric materials; piezoresponse force microscopy or related scanning-probe methods; and micro- and nanoscale mechanics, computational mechanics, or electronics packaging.

The original announcement lists a base annual salary of RMB 300,000–500,000 with research incentives and university contributions to social insurance and housing funds. It also lists pathways for eligible fellows to apply for research support and faculty appointments, plus housing and other benefits. See the Chinese announcement and BIT’s official recruitment notice for policy details.

Tenure-track faculty positions

The original announcement lists assistant and associate professor positions with base salaries of RMB 300,000 and 360,000 and startup funds of RMB 400,000 and 600,000, respectively. It also lists social insurance, housing, and supervisory provisions. See the Chinese announcement and BIT’s official recruitment notice for details.

How to apply

For postdoctoral and faculty applications, email a CV with your education, employment, research experience, publications, and awards, along with any other evidence of your qualifications. Use your name and the position you are applying for in the subject line.
