In March 2021, the Changqing Guo students' cooperative work was published in the Science Journal!

Machine-translated from the archive. Chinese original

In March 2021, Changqing Guo was warmly congratulated for his collaborative work in the Science Journal! Changqing Guo, the second author of the article, carried out a phase simulation study on the expansion of the structure of the P (VDF-TrFE) film under different state of reaction, revealing that the system energy competition influenced by the cyclical fluctuations in the surface of the film was the reason for the rise in the structure, as well as the results of the adaptation control of that structure.
