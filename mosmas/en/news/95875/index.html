Xueyun Wang receives the Symmetry Young Scientist Award

Machine-translated from the archive. Chinese original

In March 2024, Mr. Xueyun Wang was congratulated for the outstanding young scholar of Symmetry Journal!
2023 Symmetry Award for Young Scientists (global selection of 1 person, nomination of a senior expert, evaluation by the Committee of Experts)
The panel is composed of three to six members of a senior academic editorial board, including the editor-in-chief of the journal Prof. Dr. Sergei Odintsov. The candidates are to be nominated by senior scientists, and young scholars who have to earn a doctorate for up to 10 years and make a groundbreaking study that makes a significant contribution to progress in research.
List of previous awards:
2018: Diego Saez-Chillon Gomez (University of Basque, Spain)
2019 Martiros Khurshudyan (University of Science and Technology, China)
2020: Sunny Vagnozzi (University of Cambridge, United Kingdom)
2021: Tanmoy Paul (University of Bedwan, India)
2022: Yao Chen (Southeastern University)
2023: Xueyun Wang (Beijing Institute of Technology)
