Yingtao Zhao

Professor

English profile summary. Chinese original

Yingtao Zhao is a professor of mechanics in the School of Aerospace Engineering at Beijing Institute of Technology. He teaches and researches elasticity, micromechanics, and finite-element analysis. He earned a bachelor’s degree and a PhD at Peking University.

Appointments

  • Since December 2024 · Professor, Beijing Institute of Technology
  • 2015–2024 · Associate professor, Beijing Institute of Technology
  • 2004–2015 · Lecturer, Beijing Institute of Technology
  • 2014–2015 · Visiting scholar, Columbia University

Teaching and recognition

His courses include elasticity, mechanics of materials, and advances in modern mechanics. His honors include a 2023 National Teaching Achievement Award (second prize, lead contributor), a 2022 Beijing Higher Education Teaching Achievement Award (first prize, lead contributor), and the Chinese Society of Theoretical and Applied Mechanics’ 2021 Xu Zhilun Outstanding Teacher Award.
