Bonan Zhu

Tenure-track Professor · PhD Supervisor

English profile summary. Chinese original

Bonan Zhu is a professor and PhD supervisor in the School of Aerospace Engineering at Beijing Institute of Technology. He works on the theoretical design and discovery of energy and optoelectronic materials, including oxide interfaces and lithium battery materials. He develops software for high-throughput calculations and crystal structure prediction.

Education and appointments

  • Since 2023 · Professor, Beijing Institute of Technology
  • 2020–2023 · Postdoctoral fellow, University College London
  • 2016–2020 · PhD in materials, University of Cambridge
  • 2012–2016 · Undergraduate and master’s study in natural sciences (physics), University of Cambridge

Teaching and service

He teaches introductory atomistic simulation and micro- and nanoscale measurement. He is an editor at the Journal of Open Source Software.
