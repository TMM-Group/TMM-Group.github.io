Jiawang Hong

Tenured Professor · PhD Supervisor

English profile summary. Chinese original

Jiawang Hong is a tenured professor, PhD supervisor, and vice dean in the School of Aerospace Engineering at Beijing Institute of Technology. He studies multiphysics coupling in smart materials and structures, combining theory, experiment, and atomistic simulation. His research has received support from the National Natural Science Foundation of China and the National Key R&D Program.

Education and appointments

  • Since 2016 · Professor, Beijing Institute of Technology
  • 2013–2016 · Postdoctoral fellow, Oak Ridge National Laboratory
  • 2010–2013 · Postdoctoral fellow, Rutgers University
  • 2004–2010 · PhD, Tsinghua University, including a joint doctoral placement at the University of Cambridge in 2007–2008
  • 2000–2004 · Undergraduate study in engineering mechanics, Hunan University

Teaching and recognition

He teaches Scientific Computing and Programming and Advanced Materials Design and Applications. His honors include the 2025 Outstanding Young Scientist Award in Dielectric Physics (Category A) and a 2023 National Teaching Achievement Award (second prize, team member).
