Xueyun Wang

Tenure-track Professor · PhD Supervisor

English profile summary. Chinese original

Xueyun Wang is a professor and PhD supervisor in the School of Aerospace Engineering at Beijing Institute of Technology. He earned his PhD in physics at Rutgers University under Sang-Wook Cheong. He studies the formation and evolution of ferroelectric and piezoelectric domains and how coupled fields can control them.

Research

  • Multiphysics coupling in piezoelectric, ferroelectric, ferromagnetic, and multiferroic materials
  • Atomic force microscopy and coupled-field control of functional domains
  • Design and growth of functional single crystals

Teaching and recognition

He teaches partial differential equations, micro- and nanoscale measurement, and undergraduate research. He serves on the editorial board of the Journal of Advanced Dielectrics and received the 2023 Symmetry Young Scientist Award.
