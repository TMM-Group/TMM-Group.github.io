Zewei Hou

Tenure-track Associate Professor · PhD Supervisor

English profile summary. Chinese original

Zewei Hou is a tenure-track associate professor and PhD supervisor in the School of Aerospace Engineering at Beijing Institute of Technology. His theoretical and experimental work examines multiphysics coupling in functional materials, piezoelectric materials and wearable devices, acoustic tweezers, and the design of smart structures.

Education and appointments

  • Since August 2022 · Associate professor, Beijing Institute of Technology
  • 2020–2022 · Boya Postdoctoral Fellow, Peking University
  • 2014–2020 · PhD in solid mechanics, Peking University
  • 2010–2014 · Bachelor’s degree in engineering mechanics, Huazhong University of Science and Technology

Teaching and service

He teaches Mechanics of Materials and Fundamentals of Aircraft Structural Mechanics. He serves on the early-career editorial board of Extreme Mechanics Letters.
