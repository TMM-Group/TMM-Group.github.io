Gang Tang

Tenure-track Associate Professor · PhD Supervisor

English profile summary. Chinese original

Gang Tang is a tenure-track associate professor and PhD supervisor at the Advanced Research Institute of Multidisciplinary Science, Beijing Institute of Technology. He uses first-principles modelling and machine learning to study functional semiconductors. His work spans ferroelectric, piezoelectric, dielectric, optoelectronic, mechanical, and thermal properties.

Education and appointments

  • Since December 2021 · Associate professor, Beijing Institute of Technology
  • 2019–2021 · Postdoctoral researcher in condensed-matter physics, University of Liège
  • 2015–2019 · PhD in mechanics, Beijing Institute of Technology
  • 2012–2015 · Master’s degree, Shantou University, including a joint research placement at the Ningbo Institute of Materials Technology and Engineering, Chinese Academy of Sciences

Research and teaching

He studies multiscale modelling of ferroelectrics and piezoelectrics, machine learning for materials, and machine-learning force fields. His courses include Machine Learning Algorithms and Practice in Materials Science and Artificial Intelligence and Computational Science A.
